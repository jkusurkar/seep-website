Meet the Founder

Jayvant Kusurkar (J K Sir)

Founder & Program Mentor: SEEP | SHREYAS ACADEMY

Jayvant Kusurkar

With 30 years of experience in the field of education, Jayvant Kusurkar is an experienced educator, mentor, and advocate of continuous self-development. His journey in education has strengthened his belief that true success goes beyond academic knowledge: it requires the right mindset, self-discipline, self-awareness, and purposeful action.

Driven by a passion for helping people grow, he founded the Self Enhancement & Empowerment Program (SEEP) to empower individuals to recognize their hidden potential and transform it into meaningful progress.

His philosophy emphasizes that motivation alone is not enough; lasting improvement comes from translating positive thinking into consistent action. Through SEEP, he aims to help people develop better habits, improve their focus, manage their time effectively, and take greater responsibility for their personal growth.

Drawing on his extensive experience in education, Jayvant envisions a world where individuals of all ages are empowered to discover their strengths, overcome limitations, and continually strive to become better versions of themselves.

His guiding belief: “Success begins when you stop waiting for change and start taking action.”
